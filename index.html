What emerges across the responses

The recurring proposal is to automate repeatable evidence handling while retaining experienced engineers for consequential judgments. The differences are in how firms organize that work, establish independence, and staff the specialist roles.

Keep these distinctions in view

Conflict safeguards are proposals, not Government approvals. Start dates use different triggers. Remote staffing ranges from optional reach-back to a core delivery preference.
