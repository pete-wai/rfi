The responses, at a glance

Submissions repeatedly separate repeatable intake and evidence-handling work from engineering judgments about scope, effectiveness, conflicting evidence, and escalation. The important differences are organizational: pod size, senior coverage, dedicated automation, independent QA, and which roles can be combined. Ratios are planning proposals, not demonstrated capacity or Government requirements.
