On-site and remote staffing

Is there sufficient expert staffing available in the Washington DC metro area to support on-site requirements with the FedRAMP team at GSA, or is remote staffing outside the DC metro area necessary to maintain the highest quality of contractor staff?

35 submissions · Compared within this question

Summary & comparisonTechnical notesCompany answers

The responses, at a glance

Many submissions propose a DC-based leadership or review core with nationwide specialists. That shared hybrid language masks a substantive difference: KITC, Coretec, Prudent, and others say remote staff are not necessary for quality; FITS says national remote staffing is necessary; Obsidian favors telework first. These are respondent judgments and staffing claims, not an independent labor-market study.

“Hybrid” does not mean agreement on necessity

KITC and Prudent say remote staff are not necessary to maintain quality, while FITS says remote staffing is necessary. EY says both local and remote capacity are needed. Those positions should remain distinct.

Read the evidence:Kapoor IT Consulting LLC (KITC)Prudent Technology LLCFirst Information Technology ServicesErnst & Young LLP

Remote work has different boundaries

Cloud9 proposes mostly local reviewers with a small remote specialist group. Noblis uses remote staff for routine package review and documentation. EY places scalable Tier 1 processing outside DC, and Obsidian starts from a telework-first preference.

Read the evidence:Cloud9 Security Services, Inc.Noblis, Inc.Ernst & Young LLPObsidian Global, LLC

Location is also a pricing question

Secured IT Solutions, TES, and Guidehouse ask for explicit onsite cadence or notice. Their concern is not only talent availability but the ability to price attendance and commit staffing.

Read the evidence:Secured IT SolutionsThe Enterprise Security Consultants, LLC (dba TES Consultants)Guidehouse Inc.

Availability remains a claim to confirm

Softthink, Vertekal, and Guidehouse explicitly condition commitments on checking personnel availability, qualifications, independence, and attendance requirements. A local office or national bench does not by itself quantify deployable capacity.

Read the evidence:Softthink Solutions IncVertekal LLCGuidehouse Inc.

Unable to verifyLocal and national talent claims are not independently measured
