Question 8 of 8

Is there sufficient expert staffing available in the Washington DC metro area to support on-site requirements with the FedRAMP team at GSA, or is remote staffing outside the DC metro area necessary to maintain the highest quality of contractor staff?

Summary of responses

Many submissions propose a DC-based core with remote specialists or surge staff. Some say local staffing can fully meet the need; others describe remote staffing as necessary or central to their delivery model.

  • KITC and Prudent say remote staffing is not necessary; FITS says it is necessary; EY says both local and remote capacity are needed.
  • Cloud9 proposes mostly local reviewers with remote specialists; Noblis includes routine review among remote duties; Obsidian favors telework first.
  • Secured IT Solutions, TES, and Guidehouse ask for details about onsite attendance.
