Question 7 of 8

How quickly could your team begin supporting active reviews, and how would you handle changes to FedRAMP rules, schemas, or procedures during performance?

Summary of responses

Proposed starts range from immediate support to phased mobilization over several weeks. Some timelines begin at award; others begin after access, screening, or onboarding. Many answers describe version tracking, updated procedures, testing, and Government direction when rules or tools change.

  • Noblis describes day-one incumbent support; Longevity targets five business days after prerequisites; WITS targets supervised review in 45 days and full capacity in 90.
  • Coretec describes rechecking in-flight packages; Excentium describes retaining the governing baseline unless directed otherwise.
  • Cloud9, Softthink, and EY include testing revised tools or checks.
