Mobilization and change

How quickly could your team begin supporting active reviews, and how would you handle changes to FedRAMP rules, schemas, or procedures during performance?

35 submissions · Compared within this question

Summary & comparisonTechnical notesCompany answers

The responses, at a glance

Some submissions promise immediate support, others a first team within roughly ten to thirty days, and several distinguish initial reviews from full capacity at sixty to ninety days. Award, notice to proceed, access approval, and supervised production are not equivalent starting points. Most describe monitoring changes and updating controlled procedures, but the treatment of in-flight packages varies.

Normalize the clock before comparing speed

Noblis claims uninterrupted day-one incumbent support. Longevity targets five business days after access and other prerequisites. WITS targets supervised review within 45 days and full capacity by 90. These are not like-for-like mobilization promises.
